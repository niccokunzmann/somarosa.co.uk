## This week's focus

- Consent, self-responsibility and the importance of honouring your own pace.
- A trauma-aware approach, with permission to pause, adapt or opt out of any practice at any time.
- Developing body awareness and recognising internal signals of **yes**, **no** and **neutral**.
- Gentle breath, sounding and felt-sense meditation.
- Using inner awareness as a compass, inspired by Gestalt principles, to listen to the different parts and needs within yourself.

## Complete practice: Gestalt Yoni & Heart Harmonising

**Duration: 1 hour**

This Gestalt-inspired exploration invites you to listen inwardly and create a dialogue between your Yoni and your heart. Allow yourself to become curious about what each part may be holding, needing or communicating.

You might explore the relationship between feminine and masculine aspects within yourself, or simply listen to what your Yoni needs. There is no correct answer and nothing to fix. Let your own experience guide you.

This practice supports gentle awareness of your nervous system and the sensations, impulses and signals that arise in the body. Stay within a range that feels manageable. You can pause, look around the room, change position or stop whenever you choose.

## Homeplay: Breath & Body Scan

**5–10 minutes**

1. Find a position in which you feel comfortable and supported.
2. Allow your breathing to move naturally. There is no need to force or deepen it.
3. Slowly bring awareness through your body, noticing sensation, ease, tension or neutrality.
4. Notice what feels like a yes, a no or simply neutral, without needing to change anything.
5. Finish by taking a moment to notice how you feel before returning to your day.

The intention is not to achieve a particular state. It is to practise listening and build trust in your own inner awareness.

## Remember

You are the authority on your body and your experience. You never need to push through discomfort or complete a practice simply because it has been offered. Move at a pace that feels appropriate for you.

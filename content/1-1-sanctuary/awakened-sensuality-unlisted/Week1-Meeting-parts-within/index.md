<h1>Week 1: Meeting the Parts Within</h1>

<p><em>Creating safety, slowing down, and learning to listen to the wisdom of your body.</em></p>

<p>Welcome to Week One. This is an invitation to begin turning your awareness inward, gently reconnecting with the different parts of yourself and developing a deeper relationship with your body's inner landscape.</p>

<p>Before we explore pleasure, intimacy or deeper layers of embodiment, we begin with safety. We learn to slow down, recognise our boundaries, and listen to the subtle language of the body without expectation or judgement.</p>

<p>Throughout this journey, I may use words such as <em>Yoni</em> and <em>Lingam</em> as sacred expressions for our sexual anatomy. You are always welcome to use the language that feels most comfortable and resonant for you, whether that is womb, pelvic space, genitals or something entirely your own.</p>

<h2>What We'll Explore</h2>

<ul>
<li>Consent, self-responsibility and the importance of honouring your own pace.</li>
<li>A trauma-aware approach to embodiment, including the freedom to pause, modify or opt out of any practice.</li>
<li>Developing body awareness and recognising your internal signals of yes, no and neutral.</li>
<li>Gentle breathwork, sounding and felt-sense meditation to deepen your connection with the present moment.</li>
<li>Using inner awareness as a compass, inspired by Gestalt principles, to listen to the different parts and needs within yourself.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Practice 1: Gestalt Yoni &amp; Heart Harmonising</h2>

<p><strong>Duration: 1 hour</strong></p>

<p>An exploration of Gestalt principles as a pathway into deeper self-awareness, connection and inner listening.</p>

<p>Through this practice, we invite a dialogue between the Yoni and the heart, creating space to listen to what each part of you may be holding, needing or communicating.</p>

<p>Rather than trying to change or fix anything, we practise meeting ourselves with curiosity, compassion and presence. You may discover new layers of awareness around your relationship with your body, sexuality, emotions and inner feminine and masculine energies.</p>

<h2>Practice 2: Somatic Experiencing</h2>

<p><strong>Guided Practice: Recognising Safety &amp; Danger Cues</strong></p>
<p><strong>Duration: 30 minutes</strong></p>

<p>In this guided somatic practice, we begin developing awareness of how your nervous system responds to the world around you.</p>

<p>You'll be invited to gently notice the sensations, impulses and subtle signals associated with safety, activation and potential threat. Through mindful observation, we begin building the capacity to recognise what feels settling, what feels challenging, and what your body needs in each moment.</p>

<h2>Homeplay: Breath &amp; Body Awareness</h2>

<p><strong>Daily practice: 5–10 minutes</strong></p>

<p>Set aside a few moments each day to slow down and reconnect with yourself.</p>

<ul>
<li>Find a comfortable position where you feel supported.</li>
<li>Bring your awareness to your natural breathing rhythm, without needing to change it.</li>
<li>Gently scan through your body, noticing sensations, areas of tension, openness or neutrality.</li>
<li>Notice what feels comfortable, what feels uncomfortable, and what simply is.</li>
<li>Allow yourself to respond with curiosity rather than judgement.</li>
</ul>

<p>There is nothing you need to achieve. This is an opportunity to practise listening, building trust and becoming more familiar with your own inner landscape.</p>

<h2>A Gentle Reminder</h2>

<p>Move at your own pace. You are the authority on your body and your experience. You never need to push through discomfort or complete a practice simply because it has been offered.</p>

<p>We are not here to force an opening, but to cultivate the conditions in which deeper connection can unfold naturally.</p>

<p><em>Begin by listening. Everything else grows from there.</em></p>

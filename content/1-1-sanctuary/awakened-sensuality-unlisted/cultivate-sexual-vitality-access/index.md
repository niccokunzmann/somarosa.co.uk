<h1>Cultivate Sexual Vitality</h1>

<p class="course-lead">An invitation to awaken your life force, deepen your connection with your body and explore the cultivation of sexual vitality through Taoist-inspired practices.</p>

Welcome.

I'm delighted to share this guided practice with you.

Sexual vitality is about more than sexuality. It is an expression of our life force, our capacity to feel, our embodied presence and our relationship with the energy that moves through us.

Through this 40-minute guided practice, you'll be invited to slow down, turn your awareness inward and explore ways of cultivating and connecting with your sexual vitality.

## Your guided practice

**Duration: 40 minutes**

Find a quiet space where you can relax without interruption. You may wish to have a comfortable place to sit or lie down, allowing yourself enough room to move and explore.

Approach the practice with curiosity rather than expectation. There is no particular experience you need to create or outcome you need to achieve.

## Before you begin

- Choose a space where you feel comfortable and undisturbed.
- Wear clothing that allows you to breathe and move freely.
- Keep some water nearby.
- Listen to your body and honour your own pace.
- Pause, adapt or stop whenever you need to.

## A gentle reminder

Your body is your own. There is no right or wrong way to experience this practice.

Allow yourself to explore without pressure, trusting your own boundaries, sensations and capacity.

Enjoy your practice.

<h1>Grounding into Presence</h1>

<p class="course-lead">This week is an invitation to soften, slow down and bring compassion to the parts of you that may hold shame, unspoken desire or resistance. We begin gently, allowing breath and sound to support a deeper felt sense of presence.</p>

## Part 1: Softening through breath and sound

Begin with gentle breathing and sounding. Let the body soften rather than asking it to do more. Allow a felt-sense meditation to unfold at a pace that feels comfortable.

You may explore a soft **VUUUU** sound on the exhale, noticing the vibration and allowing the out-breath to lengthen naturally. There is no need to push, strain or make the sound louder than feels right.

## Guided practice: Shakti Meditation

**35 minutes**

Move from gentle VUUUU exhales into the Shakti meditation. Keep the practice soft and responsive. Let your body choose whether to make sound, remain quiet, move or rest.

## Part 2: Meeting the parts that hold you back

Our focus now turns towards bringing compassion to shame, desire and internal resistance. This is not about forcing yourself to become more open or pushing past your boundaries. It is an opportunity to listen to what is present and understand what different parts of you may need.

- Journaling about desires you have never spoken aloud.
- Exploring shame, expectation and internal pressure around sensuality.
- Using gentle sound as a way to express and release emotion, only as much as feels supportive.
- Noticing what happens in the body when you imagine allowing more pleasure.

## Guided practice: Highest Pleasure Potential

**35 minutes**

This practice moves through spinal cord breathing, the inner smile, visualisation and an exploration of the parts that may hold you back.

As you move through the meditation, you might gently enquire:

- What holds me back from my highest pleasure potential?
- What expectations, fears or inherited ideas arise when I imagine allowing myself more pleasure?
- Who would I be if I embodied my highest pleasure potential?

Allow whatever answers, sensations or emotions arise to be met with curiosity. You do not need to resolve anything in this session.

## Gentle breathwork and sounding

Return to soft breathwork, allowing the exhale to become full and unforced. If it feels supportive, let a heavier breath or sound emerge naturally. You might imagine the exhale travelling down through the Yoni and flowing like a river, releasing only what is ready to move.

Keep the practice gentle. Emotional release is not something to manufacture, and stillness or no noticeable change are equally valid experiences.

## Journaling

Give yourself time to write without editing or judging what comes through.

- What desires have I kept unspoken?
- What beliefs or expectations shape the way I relate to sensuality?
- Which parts of me feel curious about more pleasure? Which parts feel hesitant?
- What might help those hesitant parts feel respected and included?

## Homeplay

**Journaling and gentle breath**

- Continue journaling, allowing space for desires, questions and feelings to emerge without judgement.
- Breathe gently into the body, without forcing the inhale.
- On the exhale, allow the breath and any natural sound to move through the throat.
- Notice how your body responds. Pause or return to natural breathing whenever you need.

The invitation this week is softness, not pressure. Let compassion lead.

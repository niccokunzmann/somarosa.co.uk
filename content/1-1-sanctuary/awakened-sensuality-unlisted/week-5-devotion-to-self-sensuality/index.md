<h1>Devotion to Self-Sensuality</h1>

<p class="course-lead">
  This week is an invitation to approach your own body with devotion,
  tenderness and attention. Through self-touch, we create time to slow down,
  listen and experience sensuality as a relationship with ourselves.
</p>

## This week's focus

Devotional self-touch is an opportunity to bring presence and care to the
way you meet your body. Rather than treating touch as something to perform
or achieve, allow it to become an exploration of attention, sensation and
connection.

Move at a pace that feels supportive. Notice what you enjoy, what feels
nourishing and where you might want more space or gentleness.

## Guided practice: Devotional Self-Touch

Settle into a comfortable space and allow this guided practice to support
your exploration of devotional self-touch. Stay connected to your own
boundaries and let your experience unfold in its own way.

## Your home practice

Return to a simple moment of devotional self-touch during the week. Give
yourself time without rushing, and notice how it feels to offer your body
your own attention and care.

You can keep the practice brief. What matters is the quality of presence
you bring, not how long you spend or what you experience.

## A gentle reminder

There is no ideal way to practise sensuality. Let devotion mean listening
to yourself, respecting your boundaries and allowing your own needs to matter.

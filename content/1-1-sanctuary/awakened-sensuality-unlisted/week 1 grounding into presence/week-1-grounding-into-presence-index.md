## This week's focus

Our starting point is safety. Rather than asking your body to feel or experience
anything in particular, we begin by making space for what is already here.

Throughout this module, you are invited to:

- Explore consent, self-responsibility and your own pace.
- Notice the difference between sensations of **yes**, **no** and **neutral**.
- Begin recognising your body's signals without judging or trying to change them.
- Use gentle breath, sounding and felt-sense awareness to support presence.
- Develop your inner awareness as a compass, listening with curiosity rather than expectation.

There is no right way to experience this work. You are always welcome to pause,
adapt a practice, keep your eyes open, or stop altogether. Follow what feels
appropriate for you in each moment.

## Your home practice

**A gentle 5–10 minute breath and body scan**

Set aside a little time each day to pause and notice your body.

1. Find a position that feels comfortable enough for you.
2. Allow your breathing to settle naturally. There is no need to force or deepen it.
3. Slowly bring your awareness through your body, noticing areas of sensation, ease, tension or neutrality.
4. Notice whether anything feels like a yes, a no or simply neutral.
5. Finish by taking a moment to notice how you feel before returning to your day.

Keep the practice simple. The intention is not to achieve a particular state, but
to build a relationship with your own awareness, one moment at a time.

## A reminder as you begin

This is an educational and experiential course, not therapy. You are the
authority on your own experience. Please work at a pace that feels manageable,
and seek appropriate support if a practice brings up more than you feel able
to hold.

Take your time. There is nothing to force and nowhere you need to get to.

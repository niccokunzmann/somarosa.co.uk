## This week's focus

This integration session is an invitation to deepen feminine expression
through movement, voice and stillness. We draw together the practices of
felt-sense meditation, feeling into the body, breathing and sounding, allowing
each element to inform the next.

Rather than searching for a particular experience, notice what has become
more available to you across the journey. Where do you feel sensation?
Where is there expression, movement or stillness? What wants to be felt?

## Guided practice: Breathwork and Pleasure Alchemy

This breathwork practice offers space to explore the relationship between
breath, sensation and pleasure. Move gently and remain attentive to what
feels supportive for your body.

## Integration: Bringing sensual embodiment into daily life

The final part of the journey is about carrying what you have explored into
everyday life, relationships and your connection with yourself.

Consider how you might make space for small moments of embodied awareness:
pausing to feel, breathing consciously, expressing yourself through movement
or sound, and noticing what supports a sense of aliveness and connection.

## Your integration practice

Return to the practices from the previous weeks that felt meaningful or
nourishing. You might choose a few moments of conscious self-touch, pelvic
breathing, sounding, movement or stillness.

Let your practice be responsive to the day you are having. Integration is
not about doing everything. It is about recognising what you want to carry
forward and finding ways to make it part of your life.

## A final reflection

What has shifted in the way you listen to your body? What helps you feel
connected to your sensuality? How might you continue to honour pleasure,
expression and your own pace beyond this course?

Thank you for giving yourself the time and attention to make this journey.

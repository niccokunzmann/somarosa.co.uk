## Make It Part of Your Daily Practice

You don't need to wait until you have an hour to dedicate to yourself.

Try incorporating these practices into your day:

- **In the morning:** Awaken your energy and set the tone for your day.
- **During an afternoon slump:** Take five minutes to reconnect with your vitality.
- **Before a creative activity:** Cultivate your life force and bring fresh energy into what you're doing.
- **Whenever you feel depleted:** Pause, practise and reconnect.

Small moments of conscious practice can transform your relationship with your energy.

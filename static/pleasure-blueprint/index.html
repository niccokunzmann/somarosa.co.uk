The Pleasure Blueprint

Discover your unique pathways to pleasure, desire and intimacy.

A journey of self-discovery into your sensual nature. What awakens your desire? What allows you to melt into pleasure? What makes you feel deeply connected, stimulated, inspired or alive? Discover the unique ways you experience pleasure through a series of reflective questions.

Your relationship with pleasure is uniquely yours. It is shaped by your body, your nervous system, your experiences, your imagination and the ways you connect with yourself and others.

Perhaps your desire awakens through deep heart connection, or the anticipation of what might happen. Maybe it lives in fantasy and imagination, or the beauty of a carefully created sensual experience. You might need trust and emotional safety before you can fully open, or feel most alive through physical sensation, dance and embodied expression. Perhaps you are naturally versatile, moving fluidly between different experiences.

There is no single way to experience pleasure, and no one blueprint you need to fit into. The Pleasure Blueprint is an invitation to become curious about your own patterns, discover what awakens your desire and explore new possibilities for connection, sensuality and pleasure.

What will you discover?

  • What naturally awakens your desire.
  • Whether connection, anticipation, imagination, sensuality, trust or physical expression plays the greatest role in your pleasure.
  • The environments and experiences that help you relax and receive.
  • How your mind, senses and body contribute to your sensuality.
  • Whether you have a dominant pathway into pleasure or move fluidly between different experiences.

How it works

  1. Explore. Answer 14 reflective questions about your relationship with pleasure, desire and intimacy.
  2. Discover. Receive your primary Pleasure Blueprint and a secondary profile that reveals another aspect of your sensual nature.
  3. Reflect. Explore your results, understand what supports your pleasure and discover new possibilities for your sensual expression.

Ready to discover your Pleasure Blueprint?

Take a few moments for yourself. Find a comfortable space and answer from your honest experience, rather than how you think you should feel. There are no right or wrong answers, and no ideal result.

The Pleasure Blueprint is an educational self-reflection tool, not a psychological assessment or diagnosis. Your results are not fixed identities or labels. Your relationship with pleasure can evolve and change throughout your life.
